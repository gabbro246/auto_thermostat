# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Initial version of Auto Thermostat.
- Home, Away and Sleep presets with automatic Home/Away detection.
- Averaged temperature from multiple sensors.
- Timed Boost with optional short-press and long-press event button.
- Generic Thermostat cycle settings and standard climate card support.
- Fixed clearing previously selected people and Boost buttons.
- Prevented non-temperature sensors from affecting thermostat control.
